Physics Concept: Units and Measurements

Units and measurements are important because they help us describe quantities correctly. For example, food can be measured in kilograms, while land can be measured in hectares or square meters.

Units Used

  • Kilogram (kg) for food mass
  • People for population
  • Hectare (ha) for farmland area
  • Square meter (m²) for area
  • Metric tonne (t) for large food amounts

Formulas Used

Annual Food Requirement

F = P x d x 365

F = annual food requirement in kilograms
P = population
d = daily food requirement per person in kilograms
365 = number of days in one year

Food Reserve

F = F x (1 + Reserve / 100)

Farmland Area

A = F / Y

A = farmland area in hectares
F = annual food requirement with reserve
Y = annual crop yield in kilograms per hectare

Unit Conversions

1 metric tonne = 1,000 kilograms

1 hectare = 10,000 square meters

Variables Used

Independent Variable: Population (P)

Dependent Variable 1: Food requirement (F)

Dependent Variable 2: Farmland area (A)

Assumptions and Limitations

Assumptions

  • Each person needs the same amount of food per day.
  • Food needs stay the same throughout the year.
  • Crop yield is the same in all farmland.
  • The calculator uses 365 days in one year.

Limitations

  • Food needs are different for every person.
  • Crop yield depends on weather, soil, water, and farming methods.
  • Food waste, imports, and exports are not included.
  • The result is only an estimate.
